Frequently asked

Where is the app? Fish Ocean lives behind your windows. Find its 🐠 icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen for all options.

Will it slow my Mac down? It's built to be light: the art is pre-rendered, it lowers its frame rate on battery, and it stops drawing when windows cover your desktop (except while a friend's fish is visiting, so it doesn't freeze on their screen).

Is my fish saved? Yes. Its size, age and journal are kept on your Mac, even after restarting.

How do I swim with a friend? You both need to be signed in to iCloud. Open the 🐠 menu → My Friend Code, send the code to your friend, and they choose Add Friend…. With Full Ocean you can then pick Invite … to swim; your friend doesn't need Full Ocean.

My friends can't connect. Friends meet over the internet (iCloud and a secure web connection). Some strict office or school networks block this; your fish keeps working normally, and the 🐠 menu tells you when friends can't be reached. Try again from another network.

I bought the full ocean on another Mac. Choose Restore purchase in the 🐠 menu.

Start at login can be turned on or off in the 🐠 menu.
