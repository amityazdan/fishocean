Live wallpaper for Mac, no subscription

One living world instead of a video library

macOS shows moving aerial scenes on the Lock Screen and as a screen saver, but the desktop itself stays still. Live wallpaper apps fill that gap. Most of them are libraries of video loops, often on a weekly or yearly subscription.
