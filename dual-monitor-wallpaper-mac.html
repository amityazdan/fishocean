Dual monitor wallpaper for Mac

One ocean across all your displays

Most wallpaper apps play one picture or video per screen. Fish Ocean treats your displays as one ocean: the reef continues from one monitor to the next, and every few minutes the clownfish swims across to the other screen, stays a while, and comes back.

Free, including multiple displays

Crossing between displays is part of the free version. Ultrawide and multi-monitor setups are supported. To save battery and CPU, very large displays are drawn slightly softer than native resolution.

A living reef, not a loop

The fish decides where to swim, and the light follows the real sunrise and sunset for your time zone, with no location access.

It pauses where windows cover the desktop and lowers its frame rate on battery. No ads, no subscription. Full Ocean (one-time) adds nine habitats, growth over about two months and blue tang schools.
