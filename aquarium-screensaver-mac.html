Aquarium screensaver for Mac

Or a reef that lives behind your windows all day

Aquarium screensavers were a classic: walk away from your Mac and a fish tank fills the screen. Fish Ocean takes a different approach. It is a wallpaper, not a screensaver, so the reef is there whenever you glance at your desktop, not only when you're away.

A fish that decides where to swim

It isn't a looped video. The clownfish turns, drifts and explores its reef on its own. The corals are based on Smithsonian Open Access 3D scans.

Day and night, like a real tank

The light follows the real sunrise and sunset for your time zone, with no location access: rosy dawns, bright turquoise days, orange sunsets and blue nights where the water glows.

Easy on your Mac

It pauses where windows cover the desktop and lowers its frame rate on battery. No ads, no subscription. The free download is your fish's home reef; Full Ocean, a one-time purchase, adds nine habitats, a fish that grows over about two months and schools of blue tangs.
