Animated wallpaper for Mac

A moving reef, not a looping clip

Looking for a moving wallpaper for your MacBook or iMac? Fish Ocean is an animated wallpaper with one living scene: a coral reef behind your windows, where a clownfish decides where to swim.

Why it doesn't feel like a loop

Video wallpapers repeat the same clip every few seconds. In Fish Ocean the fish's path is different every time, and the light changes through the day with the real sunrise and sunset for your time zone.

Free, with a one-time upgrade

The free download is your fish's home reef, day to night, on all your displays. Full Ocean is a one-time purchase, no subscription: nine habitats, eating plankton, growth, resting in its anemone and blue tang schools. It pauses where windows cover the desktop and lowers its frame rate on battery.
